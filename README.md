## Top Time Management Utility Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Time Tracking, Pomodoro Timers & Productivity Analytics*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Time Management Utilities**. These tools help individuals and teams track working hours, manage focus sessions, analyze productivity patterns, and maintain work-life boundaries.



**Examples** include Windows Clock, Toggl Track, Clockify, RescueTime, Forest, Focus Keeper, Be Focused, Harvest, Pomodone, and TickTick (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Time management is a strong open-source domain. **Super Productivity** leads as the most comprehensive open-source task and time management tool, with **ActivityWatch** for automatic time tracking, **Kimai** for team time tracking, and **Pomatez** for Pomodoro focus . This section is heavily expanded.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Toggl Track](https://toggl.com/track/)**  

  Leading time tracking platform with one-click timers, detailed reports, and team management. Free tier for up to 5 users; paid from $9/user/month. **The most widely used commercial time tracker** .



- **[Clockify](https://clockify.me/)**  

  Free time tracking with unlimited users and projects. Paid tiers add admin features, invoicing, and scheduling. **The most generous free tier** in time tracking .



- **[RescueTime](https://www.rescuetime.com/)**  

  Automatic productivity tracking that runs in the background and categorizes app/website usage. Premium adds focus sessions and alerts. **The leading automatic time tracker** .



- **[Forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/)**  

  Gamified focus timer that grows virtual trees while you work. Available on mobile and browser extension. **The most popular gamified focus tool** .



- **[Focus Keeper](https://focuskeeper.co/)**  

  Simple Pomodoro timer with customizable intervals and productivity stats.



- **[Be Focused](https://xwavesoft.com/be-focused-pro-for-iphone-ipad-mac-os-x.html)**  

  Pomodoro timer with task management for Apple devices.



- **[Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com/)**  

  Time tracking with invoicing and reporting for freelancers and agencies. From $12/user/month .



- **[Pomodone](https://pomodoneapp.com/)**  

  Pomodoro timer integrated with task management tools like Todoist, Trello, and Jira.



- **[TickTick](https://ticktick.com/)**  

  Task management with built-in Pomodoro timer, calendar, and habit tracking.



- **[FocusTimerProBot](https://t.me/FocusTimerProBot)**  

  Free focus timer that runs as a Telegram mini app - pomodoro-style sessions, break reminders and streak stats right in chat, nothing to install. Public guide library at tg.zovo.one.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Super Productivity](https://github.com/johannesjo/super-productivity)**  

  **The most comprehensive open-source time management and task tracking tool**, MIT licensed with 10,000+ GitHub stars . Features built-in **Pomodoro timer**, time tracking with worklog export, task management with subtasks and projects, **Jira/GitHub/GitLab/OpenProject integration**, daily planning, break reminders, and **idle time detection** . Available on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS . **The de facto open-source alternative to Toggl + TickTick + Pomodone combined** — ideal for developers who want one tool for tasks, time, and focus.



- **[ActivityWatch](https://github.com/ActivityWatch/activitywatch)**  

  **The leading open-source automatic time tracker**, MPL-2.0 licensed with 11,000+ GitHub stars . **Runs entirely locally** — no cloud, no accounts, no data leaving your machine . Tracks active window, browser tabs, and AFK status automatically . Features **categorization rules**, daily/weekly reports, and a web UI . Cross-platform (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android beta) with browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox . **The de facto open-source alternative to RescueTime** — privacy-first with full data ownership.



- **[Kimai](https://github.com/kimai/kimai)**  

  **The leading open-source web-based multi-user time tracker**, MIT licensed with 4,000+ GitHub stars . Works for freelancers, companies, and organizations of any size . Track times, generate reports, create invoices, and manage teams . **Self-hosted with no user limits** . Available as SaaS at kimai.cloud. **The de facto open-source alternative to Toggl for teams** — full data ownership with invoicing and reporting.



- **[Pomatez](https://github.com/zidoro/pomatez)**  

  **Beautiful, customizable Pomodoro timer** built with Electron and React, MIT licensed with 5,000+ GitHub stars . Features **always-on-top mode**, task lists, **strict mode** (prevents skipping breaks), and **customizable timer durations** . Available on Windows, macOS, and Linux . **The best-looking open-source Pomodoro timer** — ideal for focused work sessions.



- **[GTG (Getting Things GNOME)](https://github.com/getting-things-gnome/gtg)**  

  **GNOME-native task and time management** for Linux desktops, GPL-3.0 licensed . Features task organization, due dates, tags, subtasks, and **Pomodoro timer** . **Best for GNOME users** wanting native desktop integration.



- **[Watson](https://github.com/TailorDev/Watson)**  

  **Command-line time tracker** written in Python, MIT licensed . Features start/stop timers, project tagging, and **report generation** with daily/weekly/monthly summaries . **Best for developers who prefer terminal workflows** — lightweight and scriptable.



- **[timetrace](https://github.com/dominikbraun/timetrace)**  

  **Simple CLI for tracking working time**, written in Go, MIT licensed . Features start/stop timers, project organization, and JSON export . **The simplest terminal-based time tracker** — ideal for quick logging without overhead.



- **[Taskwarrior](https://github.com/GothenburgBitFactory/taskwarrior)**  

  **Powerful command-line task manager** with time tracking capabilities, MIT licensed . Features projects, tags, due dates, priorities, and **time tracking reports** . **The most capable CLI task manager** — extensive customization and plugin ecosystem.



- **[Timewarrior](https://github.com/GothenburgBitFactory/timewarrior)**  

  **Companion time tracking tool to Taskwarrior**, MIT licensed . Features start/stop tracking, tagging, and **detailed reports** with intervals and totals . **Best paired with Taskwarrior** for a complete CLI time management workflow.



- **[Gestimer](https://github.com/soffes/Gestimer)**  

  **Menu bar timer for macOS**, MIT licensed . Simple drag-to-set timer for reminders and focus sessions . **Best for quick, lightweight timers** without leaving your workflow.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **gnome-pomodoro** — GNOME Shell extension for Pomodoro timing with desktop integration .

- **Pomo** — Simple CLI Pomodoro timer for terminal users .

- **TomatoBar** — macOS menu bar Pomodoro timer with minimal design .

- **Flow** — macOS menu bar Pomodoro timer with session tracking and statistics .

- **Focus** — macOS menu bar Pomodoro timer with customizable intervals .

- **Pomodorolm** — Cross-platform Pomodoro timer with Tauri and React .

- **FocusTide** — Pomodoro timer with time tracking analytics and daily/weekly overviews .



**Frameworks for building custom time management solutions**: Combine **Super Productivity** for an all-in-one task + time + Pomodoro solution with integrations , **ActivityWatch** for automatic background tracking with privacy , and **Kimai** for team time tracking with invoicing . Use **Pomatez** for beautiful Pomodoro focus sessions or **Watson**/**timetrace** for CLI-based time logging . For task management, **Taskwarrior** + **Timewarrior** provide the most powerful CLI combination . Note that true enterprise time tracking with payroll integration, client billing, and compliance reporting remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong task management, automatic tracking, and focus timer foundations that require integration for complete time management workflows.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Time tracking tools can collect sensitive data about work habits and productivity. **ActivityWatch runs entirely locally** — no cloud transmission by design . Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening and access controls.

- **Automatic time trackers may capture sensitive window titles or URLs** — review categorization rules and privacy settings before deployment.

- **Pomodoro timers are productivity aids, not medical advice** — the 25/5 interval is a convention, not a scientifically mandated protocol .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong task management, automatic tracking, and focus timer foundations, but **enterprise payroll integration, client billing, and compliance reporting** remain primarily commercial offerings.
